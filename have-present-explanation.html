HAVE VERB – PRESENT

HAVE VERB (PRESENT) என்றால் என்ன?

Present tense-ல் HAVE / HAS என்பது ஒருவர் அல்லது ஒரு பொருளிடம் ஏதாவது இருக்கிறது / வைத்திருக்கிறார் என்பதை சொல்ல பயன்படும்.

I / You / We / They ➔ HAVE He / She / It ➔ HAS

1. POSITIVE (+)

Explanation: Present Positive sentence என்பது தற்போது ஒருவர் அல்லது ஒரு பொருளிடம் ஏதாவது இருக்கிறது என்பதை சொல்ல பயன்படும்.
Formula: Subject + HAVE / HAS + Object
Subject HAVE Verb Example Tamil Meaning
I HAVE I have a book. என்னிடம் ஒரு புத்தகம் இருக்கிறது.
He HAS He has a bike. அவனிடம் ஒரு பைக் இருக்கிறது.
She HAS She has a bag. அவளிடம் ஒரு பை இருக்கிறது.
It HAS It has a name. அதற்கு ஒரு பெயர் இருக்கிறது.
You HAVE You have a car. உங்களிடம் ஒரு கார் இருக்கிறது.
We HAVE We have a house. எங்களிடம் ஒரு வீடு இருக்கிறது.
They HAVE They have a teacher. அவர்களிடம் ஒரு ஆசிரியர் இருக்கிறார்.
💡 Simple Note:
• HAVE = இருக்கிறது / இருக்கின்றன
• HAS = இருக்கிறது / வைத்திருக்கிறார்
• 📌 Important: He / She / It ➔ HAS  |  I / You / We / They ➔ HAVE

2. NEGATIVE (-)

Explanation: Present Negative sentence என்பது தற்போது ஒருவர் அல்லது ஒரு பொருளிடம் ஏதாவது இல்லை என்பதை சொல்ல பயன்படும்.
Formula: Subject + DO / DOES + NOT + HAVE + Object
Subject Helping Verb Example Tamil Meaning
I DO NOT HAVE I do not have a book. என்னிடம் ஒரு புத்தகம் இல்லை.
He DOES NOT HAVE He does not have a bike. அவனிடம் ஒரு பைக் இல்லை.
She DOES NOT HAVE She does not have a bag. அவளிடம் ஒரு பை இல்லை.
It DOES NOT HAVE It does not have a name. அதற்கு ஒரு பெயர் இல்லை.
You DO NOT HAVE You do not have a car. உங்களிடம் ஒரு கார் இல்லை.
We DO NOT HAVE We do not have a house. எங்களிடம் ஒரு வீடு இல்லை.
They DO NOT HAVE They do not have a teacher. அவர்களிடம் ஒரு ஆசிரியர் இல்லை.
✂️ Short Forms (DO NOT = DON'T | DOES NOT = DOESN'T):
• Examples: I don't have a book. | He doesn't have a bike. | They don't have a teacher.
⚠️ IMPORTANT RULE:
After DO / DOES, always use HAVE, not HAS.
DOES ஏற்கனவே Present tense-ஐக் கவனித்துக் கொள்வதால், HAVE தனது base form-ல் இருக்கும்.

✅ He does not have a bike.  |  ❌ He does not has a bike.
✅ She doesn't have a bag.  |  ❌ She doesn't has a bag.

3. POSITIVE QUESTION (+?)

Explanation: Present Positive Question என்பது தற்போது ஒருவர் அல்லது ஒரு பொருளிடம் ஏதாவது இருக்கிறதா என்று கேட்க பயன்படும்.
📌 Important rule: DO / DOES sentence-ன் ஆரம்பத்தில் வரும்.
Formula: DO / DOES + Subject + HAVE + Object?
Helping Verb Subject Question Tamil Meaning
DO I Do I have a book? என்னிடம் ஒரு புத்தகம் இருக்கிறதா?
DOES He Does he have a bike? அவனிடம் ஒரு பைக் இருக்கிறதா?
DOES She Does she have a bag? அவளிடம் ஒரு பை இருக்கிறதா?
DOES It Does it have a name? அதற்கு ஒரு பெயர் இருக்கிறதா?
DO You Do you have a car? உங்களிடம் ஒரு கார் இருக்கிறதா?
DO We Do we have a house? நம்மிடம் ஒரு வீடு இருக்கிறதா?
DO They Do they have a teacher? அவர்களிடம் ஒரு ஆசிரியர் இருக்கிறாரா?
🔄 Simple Pattern Comparison:
• Statement: I have a book. ➔ Question: Do I have a book?
• Statement: He has a bike. ➔ Question: Does he have a bike?
📌 Important Rule: After DO / DOES, always use HAVE.
✅ Does he have a bike?
❌ Does he has a bike?

4. NEGATIVE QUESTION (-?)

Explanation: Present Negative Question என்பது தற்போது ஒருவர் அல்லது ஒரு பொருளிடம் ஏதாவது இல்லையா என்று கேட்க பயன்படும்.
Formula: DO / DOES + Subject + NOT + HAVE + Object?
Helping Verb Subject Question Tamil Meaning
DO I Do I not have a book? என்னிடம் ஒரு புத்தகம் இல்லையா?
DOES He Does he not have a bike? அவனிடம் ஒரு பைக் இல்லையா?
DOES She Does she not have a bag? அவளிடம் ஒரு பை இல்லையா?
DOES It Does it not have a name? அதற்கு ஒரு பெயர் இல்லையா?
DO You Do you not have a car? உங்களிடம் ஒரு கார் இல்லையா?
DO We Do we not have a house? நம்மிடம் ஒரு வீடு இல்லையா?
DO They Do they not have a teacher? அவர்களிடம் ஒரு ஆசிரியர் இல்லையா?
⚡ Common Short Forms:
• Don't I...?  |  • Doesn't he...?  |  • Doesn't she...?  |  • Doesn't it...?
• Don't you...?  |  • Don't we...?  |  • Don't they...?

Example: Does he not have a bike? ➔ Short form: Doesn't he have a bike? (DO NOT → DON'T | DOES NOT → DOESN'T)
📌 Important: After DO / DOES / DON'T / DOESN'T, use HAVE, not HAS.
✅ Doesn't he have a bike?
❌ Doesn't he has a bike?

🧠 Very Important

Positive sentence:
• I / You / We / They ➔ HAVE
• He / She / It ➔ HAS

But when DO / DOES is used:
• DO / DOES + HAVE

Examples:
• ✅ He has a bike.
• ✅ Does he have a bike?
• ❌ Does he has a bike?

• ✅ She has a bag.
• ✅ She does not have a bag.
• ❌ She does not has a bag.

📋 FINAL SUMMARY

Type Formula Example
Positive (+) Subject + HAVE/HAS + Object I have a book.
Negative (-) Subject + DO/DOES + NOT + HAVE + Object I do not have a book.
Positive Question (+?) DO/DOES + Subject + HAVE + Object? Do I have a book?
Negative Question (-?) DO/DOES + Subject + NOT + HAVE + Object? Do I not have a book?
🧠 நினைவில் வைத்துக்கொள்ளுங்கள்:
  • • Present HAVE = HAVE / HAS
  • • I / You / We / They ➔ HAVE
  • • He / She / It ➔ HAS
  • • Positive ➔ HAVE / HAS
  • • Negative ➔ DO / DOES + NOT + HAVE
  • • Question ➔ DO / DOES + Subject + HAVE?
  • • Negative Question ➔ DO / DOES + Subject + NOT + HAVE?
  • 📌 Final easy memory: Present ➔ HAVE / HAS  |  Past ➔ HAD  |  Future ➔ WILL HAVE
  • 🌟 Golden Rule:
    He / She / It ➔ HAS
    But: Does + HAVE  |  Doesn't + HAVE

    ✅ He has a bike.  |  ✅ Does he have a bike?  |  ✅ He does not have a bike.
    ❌ Does he has a bike?  |  ❌ He does not has a bike.
