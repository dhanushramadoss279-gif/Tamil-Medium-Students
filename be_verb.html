BE VERB

am → is → are → was → were → will be

💡 WHAT IS BE VERB?

BE Verb is used to describe a person, place, thing, condition, state, identity, age, location, etc.
BE Verb என்பது ஒருவர் அல்லது ஒரு பொருள் எப்படி இருக்கிறது, எங்கே இருக்கிறது, யார் என்பதைச் சொல்ல பயன்படுத்தப்படுகிறது.

Present Tense

am / is / are

Past Tense

was / were

Future Tense

will be

📘 SUBJECT-WISE BE VERB

PRESENT TENSE

I → am
நான் இருக்கிறேன்
You → are
நீ இருக்கிறாய்
He → is
அவன் இருக்கிறான்
She → is

அது இருக்கிறது
We → are
நாம் இருக்கிறோம்
They → are
அவர்கள் இருக்கிறார்கள்

📊 MAIN BE VERB TABLE

Tense Subject Positive (+) Positive Question (?) Negative (-) Negative Question (?-)
PRESENT TENSE
PRESENT I I am Am I? I am not Am I not?
PRESENT He / She / It He is Is he? He is not Is he not?
PRESENT You / We / They You are Are you? You are not Are you not?
PAST TENSE
PAST I / He / She / It I was Was I? I was not Was I not?
PAST You / We / They You were Were you? You were not Were you not?
FUTURE TENSE
FUTURE All Subjects I will be Will I be? I will not be Won't I be?

→ அவள் வீட்டில் இருக்கிறாள்.

→ நாம் தயாராக இருக்கிறோம்.

→ அவர்கள் நண்பர்கள்.
