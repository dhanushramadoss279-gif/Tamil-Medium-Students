HAVE VERB – PAST

HAVE VERB (PAST) என்றால் என்ன?

Past tense-ல் HAVE என்பது கடந்த காலத்தில் ஒருவர் அல்லது ஒரு பொருளிடம் ஏதாவது இருந்தது என்பதை சொல்ல பயன்படும்.

Past HAVE = HAD ALL Subjects (I, He, She, It, You, We, They) ➔ HAD

1. POSITIVE (+)

Explanation: Past Positive sentence என்பது கடந்த காலத்தில் ஒருவர் அல்லது ஒரு பொருளிடம் ஏதாவது இருந்தது என்பதை சொல்ல பயன்படும்.
Formula: Subject + HAD + Object
Subject HAVE Verb Example Tamil Meaning
I HAD I had a book. என்னிடம் ஒரு புத்தகம் இருந்தது.
He HAD He had a bike. அவனிடம் ஒரு பைக் இருந்தது.
She HAD She had a bag. அவளிடம் ஒரு பை இருந்தது.
It HAD It had a name. அதற்கு ஒரு பெயர் இருந்தது.
You HAD You had a car. உங்களிடம் ஒரு கார் இருந்தது.
We HAD We had a house. எங்களிடம் ஒரு வீடு இருந்தது.
They HAD They had a teacher. அவர்களிடம் ஒரு ஆசிரியர் இருந்தார்.
💡 Simple Note:
• HAD = இருந்தது / இருந்தான் / இருந்தாள் / இருந்தோம் / இருந்தார்கள்
• 📌 Important: HAD is the same for all subjects.

2. NEGATIVE (-)

Explanation: Past Negative sentence என்பது கடந்த காலத்தில் ஒருவர் அல்லது ஒரு பொருளிடம் ஏதாவது இல்லை என்பதை சொல்ல பயன்படும்.
Formula: Subject + DID NOT + HAVE + Object
Subject Helping Verb Example Tamil Meaning
I DID NOT HAVE I did not have a book. என்னிடம் ஒரு புத்தகம் இல்லை.
He DID NOT HAVE He did not have a bike. அவனிடம் ஒரு பைக் இல்லை.
She DID NOT HAVE She did not have a bag. அவளிடம் ஒரு பை இல்லை.
It DID NOT HAVE It did not have a name. அதற்கு ஒரு பெயர் இல்லை.
You DID NOT HAVE You did not have a car. உங்களிடம் ஒரு கார் இல்லை.
We DID NOT HAVE We did not have a house. எங்களிடம் ஒரு வீடு இல்லை.
They DID NOT HAVE They did not have a teacher. அவர்களிடம் ஒரு ஆசிரியர் இல்லை.
✂️ Short Forms (DID NOT = DIDN'T):
• Examples: I didn't have a book. | He didn't have a bike. | They didn't have a teacher.
⚠️ IMPORTANT RULE:
After DID NOT, always use HAVE, not HAD.
DID ஏற்கனவே Past tense-ஐக் காட்டுவதால், HAVE தனது base form-ல் இருக்கும்.

✅ He did not have a bike.  |  ❌ He did not had a bike.
✅ They did not have a house.  |  ❌ They did not had a house.

3. POSITIVE QUESTION (+?)

Explanation: Past Positive Question என்பது கடந்த காலத்தில் ஒருவர் அல்லது ஒரு பொருளிடம் ஏதாவது இருந்ததா என்று கேட்க பயன்படும்.
📌 Important rule: DID sentence-ன் ஆரம்பத்தில் வரும்.
Formula: DID + Subject + HAVE + Object?
Helping Verb Subject Question Tamil Meaning
DID I Did I have a book? என்னிடம் ஒரு புத்தகம் இருந்ததா?
DID He Did he have a bike? அவனிடம் ஒரு பைக் இருந்ததா?
DID She Did she have a bag? அவளிடம் ஒரு பை இருந்ததா?
DID It Did it have a name? அதற்கு ஒரு பெயர் இருந்ததா?
DID You Did you have a car? உங்களிடம் ஒரு கார் இருந்ததா?
DID We Did we have a house? நம்மிடம் ஒரு வீடு இருந்ததா?
DID They Did they have a teacher? அவர்களிடம் ஒரு ஆசிரியர் இருந்தாரா?
🔄 Simple Pattern Comparison:
• Statement: I had a book. ➔ Question: Did I have a book?
📌 Important Rule: After DID, always use HAVE.
✅ Did he have a bike?
❌ Did he had a bike?

4. NEGATIVE QUESTION (-?)

Explanation: Past Negative Question என்பது கடந்த காலத்தில் ஒருவர் அல்லது ஒரு பொருளிடம் ஏதாவது இல்லையா என்று கேட்க பயன்படும்.
Formula: DID + Subject + NOT + HAVE + Object?
Helping Verb Subject Question Tamil Meaning
DID I Did I not have a book? என்னிடம் ஒரு புத்தகம் இல்லையா?
DID He Did he not have a bike? அவனிடம் ஒரு பைக் இல்லையா?
DID She Did she not have a bag? அவளிடம் ஒரு பை இல்லையா?
DID It Did it not have a name? அதற்கு ஒரு பெயர் இல்லையா?
DID You Did you not have a car? உங்களிடம் ஒரு கார் இல்லையா?
DID We Did we not have a house? நம்மிடம் ஒரு வீடு இல்லையா?
DID They Did they not have a teacher? அவர்களிடம் ஒரு ஆசிரியர் இல்லையா?
⚡ Common Short Forms:
• Didn't I...?  |  • Didn't he...?  |  • Didn't she...?  |  • Didn't it...?
• Didn't you...?  |  • Didn't we...?  |  • Didn't they...?

Example: Did he not have a bike? ➔ Short form: Didn't he have a bike? (DID NOT → DIDN'T)
📌 Important: After DID / DIDN'T, use HAVE, not HAD.
✅ Didn't he have a bike?
❌ Didn't he had a bike?

🧠 Very Important

• Positive sentence ➔ HAD
• Negative sentence ➔ DID NOT HAVE
• Question ➔ DID + HAVE
• Negative Question ➔ DID + NOT + HAVE

Compare:
• Positive: He had a bike.
• Negative: He did not have a bike.
• Question: Did he have a bike?
• Negative Question: Did he not have a bike?
🌟 Golden Rule: DID வந்தால் HAVE தான் வரும்.
❌ Did he had? ➔ ✅ Did he have?
❌ He did not had. ➔ ✅ He did not have.

📋 FINAL SUMMARY

Type Formula Example
Positive (+) Subject + HAD + Object I had a book.
Negative (-) Subject + DID NOT + HAVE + Object I did not have a book.
Positive Question (+?) DID + Subject + HAVE + Object? Did I have a book?
Negative Question (-?) DID + Subject + NOT + HAVE + Object? Did I not have a book?
🧠 நினைவில் வைத்துக்கொள்ளுங்கள்:
  • • Past HAVE = HAD
  • • I / He / She / It / You / We / They ➔ HAD
  • • Positive ➔ HAD
  • • Negative ➔ DID NOT HAVE
  • • Question ➔ DID + Subject + HAVE?
  • • Negative Question ➔ DID + Subject + NOT + HAVE?
  • 📌 Final easy memory: Present ➔ HAVE / HAS  |  Past ➔ HAD  |  Future ➔ WILL HAVE
  • 🌟 Golden Rule: Positive-ல் ➔ HAD  |  DID வந்தால் ➔ HAVE
    ✅ He had a bike.  |  ✅ Did he have a bike?  |  ✅ He did not have a bike.
    ❌ Did he had a bike?  |  ❌ He did not had a bike.
